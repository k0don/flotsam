Bass Guitar Reference

Overview

  • Interval Map

    interval-map/

    A 4-string fretboard (frets 0–12) with every note colored and labeled by its interval from the root you pick. Hover a note for its interval name and character; a legend lists all twelve intervals with their half-step counts.

  • Intervals

    intervals/

    Cards for the 13 intervals from unison to octave, filterable by quality (perfect, major, minor, tritone). Tap a card to see its frequency ratio, character, examples from C, songs it’s recognizable in, and a keyboard diagram.

  • Triads

    triads/

    Major, minor, diminished and augmented triads in all 12 keys, plotted across the neck to fret 15. Pick a shape card — 3-string, 1 low · 2 high, or 2 low · 1 high, in root position or either inversion — to highlight everywhere that fingering fits.

  • Note Randomizer

    note-randomizer/

    A new practice set each day: a random root plus a note pool (pentatonics, triad + 6 or b7, dominant chord tones, optional modes) mapped onto the neck with a home zone and drone suggestion. Includes reroll, a session timer, and a day-streak calendar.
